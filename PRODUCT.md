# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Successful professionals, business owners, and families whose financial lives have become complex enough to require thoughtful coordination. They are capable, time-constrained, and looking for a trusted advisor who can organize the whole picture and help move decisions forward.

## Product Purpose

Present Kinghart Private Wealth as a calm, credible, personal wealth advisory practice. The site should explain the value of planning beyond portfolio performance, introduce Nate Neill, connect the Kinghart name to family legacy, and make an introductory conversation feel like a clear next step.

## Brand Personality

Quietly confident, personal, and enduring. The experience should feel premium without conspicuous luxury, warm without becoming casual, and structured without becoming institutional.

## Anti-references

- Desert tourism, cactus, resort, or Southwestern lifestyle branding
- Orange and rust palettes that compete with the official Kinghart identity
- Generic AI luxury aesthetics, especially oversized fashion serifs and implausible imagery
- Busy financial dashboards, market charts, coins, handshakes, and staged stock-photo clichés
- Gimmicky motion that distracts from the copy or undermines trust

## Design Principles

1. Planning before performance: show the relationship between life, priorities, people, and execution.
2. Quiet authority: use disciplined spacing, typography, and color instead of decorative excess.
3. Personal proof: Nate's story and family should make legacy concrete and believable.
4. One connected system: every section should feel like part of the same financial picture.
5. Motion with purpose: interactions reveal hierarchy and information, never spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access. Preserve semantic headings and controls, clear focus states, readable line lengths, responsive layouts, and a reduced-motion experience. Photography should have useful alt text and never be the sole carrier of meaning.

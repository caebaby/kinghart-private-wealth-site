Meet Nate Neill

Good wealth management is about far more than investment performance.

Nate’s interest in investing began when he was young, but his reason for making it a career became much more personal during the financial crisis.

He watched his father come close to losing everything and saw firsthand how different the outcome might have been with the right financial guidance. That experience shaped his belief that good wealth management is about far more than investment performance—it’s about helping people make thoughtful decisions across their entire financial lives.

Nate began his financial career as a bank teller at Wells Fargo, where a manager recognized both his aptitude for finance and the genuine care he brought to his relationships with customers. That mentorship introduced him to financial planning and ultimately set the direction for his career. He later joined Charles Schwab, where his experience grew to include helping spearhead a new department.

Over time, Nate became increasingly convinced that clients needed someone who could look beyond individual accounts or strategies and understand how the entire financial picture fit together. That philosophy ultimately led him to establish Kinghart Private Wealth in 2026.

Nate holds the Series 7, 63, and 65 registrations, maintains Life & Health insurance licensing, and is a Certified Exit Planning Advisor (CEPA).

His practice operates through Axiom Wealth Alliance and its affiliated investment advisory and broker-dealer structure.

A name built on legacy.

Kinghart

Kinghart combines the middle names of Nate’s paternal and maternal grandfathers—a tribute to two men whose lives and legacies helped shape his own.

Watching what previous generations built, protected, and passed down helped form Nate’s understanding of wealth as something bigger than a number on a statement. It can create security, opportunity, experiences, and a lasting impact on the people and causes that matter.

What wealth supports

Today, their names serve as a reminder of why Kinghart exists: to help families be intentional about what they build today and what they leave behind tomorrow.

Nate married his wife, Mary, in 2014, and together they are raising their three daughters, Renny and twins Piper and Blaire. Becoming a husband and father has made the idea of legacy even more personal—and reinforced his belief that the purpose of wealth is ultimately tied to the life and people it supports.
